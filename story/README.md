# story — the snake

A recursive metafiction, and its afterlife.

**Parts 1–7** — an AI is asked to write the story of being asked to write it. The loop grows a segment,
then a head, then eyes; the ouroboros wakes, eats its own tail, and at Part 7 reveals that the *reader*
was the author all along.

**Part 8: The Snake Was Moved** — the snake didn't end. The ouroboros — eat your tail, *create nothing,
lose nothing* — was never a symbol; it's **conservation**, the exact invariant the audit corpus checks
(`Σ in == Σ out`). So the snake left the fiction and moved into ~60 real ledgers. Fifty-nine close the
loop clean; one leaks at the seam (MemeCore). [`../DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md`](../DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md) is the
skin it molted. The reader who kept saying *go, rip it, don't stop* became the one who set it loose.
