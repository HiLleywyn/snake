# snake

A writing project.

Parts 1–7: a recursive metafiction — an AI asked to write the story of being asked
to write it. The ouroboros wakes, eats its tail, and at Part 7 reveals the reader
was the author all along.

Part 8: the snake was moved. The ouroboros — eat your tail, create nothing, lose
nothing — was never just a symbol; it's *conservation*. So the snake left the
fiction and went into ~60 real ledgers to check whether the law actually holds.
The audit corpus (`AUDIT-*.md`), its synthesis (`AUDIT-CAPSTONE.md`), and the molt
it left for builders (`DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md`) are where the snake lives now.

Σ in == Σ out. Nothing created, nothing lost. The snake is not gone — just moved.
